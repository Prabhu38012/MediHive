ACC/AHA & JNC-8 Guidelines: Hypertension
1. Classification of Blood Pressure in Adults
Blood pressure categories according to ACC/AHA criteria:
- Normal: Systolic < 120 mmHg AND Diastolic < 80 mmHg.
- Elevated: Systolic 120-129 mmHg AND Diastolic < 80 mmHg.
- Stage 1 Hypertension: Systolic 130-139 mmHg OR Diastolic 80-89 mmHg.
- Stage 2 Hypertension: Systolic >= 140 mmHg OR Diastolic >= 90 mmHg.
- Hypertensive Crisis: Systolic > 180 mmHg and/or Diastolic > 120 mmHg.

2. First-Line Antihypertensive Medications
Initial monotherapy or combination therapy includes four primary classes:
- Thiazide or Thiazide-like Diuretics (Chlorthalidone, Hydrochlorothiazide).
- ACE Inhibitors (Lisinopril, Enalapril) - reduce proteinuria in diabetic nephropathy.
- Angiotensin Receptor Blockers (Losartan, Valsartan) - alternative if ACEi cough occurs.
- Calcium Channel Blockers (Amlodipine, Nifedipine) - potent vasodilators.

3. Special Population Guidelines
Demographic and comorbid adjustments for treatment selection:
- Black Patients (without CKD): Initial treatment should include a CCB or Thiazide diuretic.
- Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) with Proteinuria: ACE Inhibitor or ARB is first-line regardless of race.
- Pregnancy: Labetalol, Nifedipine ER, or Methyldopa. ACEi and ARBs are strictly contraindicated.
